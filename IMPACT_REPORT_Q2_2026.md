## Executive Summary

Code Orange Dev School is the only structured Bitcoin developer education program in Southeast Asia. In 15 months of operation, we have trained 33+ developers through technical cohorts, tracked 18+ pull requests to Bitcoin open-source projects (12+ merged), built a 574-member developer community spanning 8 countries, and delivered 60+ hands-on workshops.

Our developer pipeline converts curious Bitcoiners into open-source contributors:

```
Reading Club → Sovereign Bitcoiner → Bitcoin Dojo → rawBit → Decoding Bitcoin → Privacy Track → Fellowship → OSS Contribution
```

Based at Bitcoin House Bali (Indonesia), we operate as part of the Bitcoin Indonesia ecosystem - the largest Bitcoin-only community in Asia (~55,000 Bitcoiners, 40+ monthly meetups across 40 Indonesian cities).

All curriculum is CC0-licensed and published at [github.com/code-orange-dev](https://github.com/code-orange-dev).
